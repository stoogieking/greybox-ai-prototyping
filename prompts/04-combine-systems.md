# 04 · Combine systems

Separate mechanics can produce ideas you wouldn't plan on paper. Grabbable enemies plus enemies that shoot gives you a human shield. Build each system on its own first, then ask for the combination.

---

```
Attached is [PROTOTYPE NAME]. It has two systems that work on their own:
- [System A, e.g. grabbing a staggered enemy]
- [System B, e.g. Gunners that fire bolts at the player]

Make them interact in ONE way:
[e.g. "A bolt that hits a grabbed enemy damages that enemy instead of the player."]

Expose the numbers as sliders. Don't change either system otherwise.
After the code, suggest 3 more interactions between these two systems, in one line each. Don't build them.
```

Pick one suggestion, play it, keep it or throw it away.
